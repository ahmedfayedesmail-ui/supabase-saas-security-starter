The risk

Multi-tenant SaaS has one rule you cannot casually break.

Tenant A must not read Tenant B’s data. RLS can enforce that boundary, but a policy is easier to trust when the important access paths are also written down and tested.

“Security verification is part of the product proposition — not a blanket claim that every application is secure.”
